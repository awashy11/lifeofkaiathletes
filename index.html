Load, travel, selection pressure and a public identity tied to results. The margins that decide careers are often the ones nobody is coaching.

The method

Two practices. One steady body.

Taha Tinana, the body, sits at the centre. Breath tunes the nervous system. Kai builds the foundation it runs on.

Pillar one

Breathwork

Simple, repeatable protocols you can use in the changing room, on the bus or in bed.

  • Pre-performance calm and focus
  • Post-session downshift to speed up recovery
  • Wind-down practice for deeper sleep
  • Tools for pressure moments, on and off the field
Pillar two

Kai

Eating from the whenua and the moana: real food, prepared with intention, fitted to your training week.

  • Practical kai plans around training and travel
  • Kitchen confidence, with meals you can make fast
  • Whole-food fuelling for energy and recovery
  • A healthier relationship with food, not another rulebook

The framework

Four walls of one whare.

Te Whare Tapa Whā sees wellbeing as four connected dimensions. Strong athletes are built on all four, and when one wall weakens, the whole house feels it.

Taha Tinana · Body · The centre

Breath & kai

Where we begin: breathwork, food, sleep, movement and the nervous system.

Taha Hinengaro · Mind

Clarity under pressure

Handling expectation, setbacks and the noise between games.

Taha Whānau · Connection

Your people

Team, family and community. The belonging that holds you when form dips.

Taha Wairua · Spirit

Who you are beyond the jersey

Purpose, values and identity that outlast any season.

Questions

Good to know.

Will this fit around my training load?

Yes. Practices are short and designed to slot into the day you already have, including travel and game weeks.

Do I need to change my whole diet?

No. We work with what you already do and build up from there. The focus is on real food and confidence in the kitchen.

Is this only for individuals?

Individuals, squads and clubs are all welcome. Get in touch to talk about what suits your team.

Is this a replacement for medical or sports-science support?

No. It works alongside your existing medical, nutrition and performance staff, and doesn’t replace them.
